## Thesis

The market is pricing GLD as a liquid, low-friction proxy for physical gold at a moment when investors want ballast against currency and rate uncertainty — the shares are up 23.1% over the past year, and net income (mark-to-market gold appreciation, not operating profit) of $23.17B on $130.10B of trust assets reflects that bullion re-rating flowing straight through to NAV. Bulls are effectively underwriting continued demand for gold as a reserve/hedge asset, expressed here because GLD offers exchange liquidity without vaulting logistics.

For that to keep working, spot gold itself has to keep appreciating or at minimum hold its gains — GLD has no revenue line, no margin, and no earnings power independent of the metal it holds (hence the "n/a" on P/E, EPS, and revenue in this filing set), so the entire thesis collapses to a single variable: the gold price path. Anything that reduces the opportunity cost trade-off in gold's favor (falling real rates, dollar weakness, central-bank buying) supports the position; anything that reverses it does not.
