## Thesis

The market is pricing Apple as a re-accelerating growth story rather than a mature hardware franchise: the stock sits just 1% off its 52-week high after a 47.4% one-year run, and the June-quarter print gave bulls plenty of cover — $109.4B revenue (+16% YoY), records in iPhone, Mac and Services, an all-time-high installed base, and diluted EPS of $2.02 (+29% YoY). Layer on the Siri AI relaunch at WWDC26 and reported 2027 foldable iPhone / camera-equipped AirPods plans, and the thesis is that Apple is entering a multi-year upgrade and services-attach cycle broad enough to justify a 35.7x trailing P/E on a company already generating $128.93B of TTM net income at a 27.6% net margin.

For that to work, the growth needs to be structural, not borrowed from one-off items: management itself disclosed that ~2 points of the 50.1% gross margin and $0.11 of the $2.02 EPS came from tariff refunds, so the underlying quarter was closer to 48% gross margin and roughly $1.91 EPS. The bull case requires Services (already a record $30.7B in the quarter) to keep growing despite the EU's Digital Markets Act forcing a revamp of App Store fees, and it requires the foldable iPhone / AirPods roadmap to actually ship on schedule in 2027 and drive a unit upgrade cycle large enough to keep 16%+ topline growth going without more tariff-refund-style tailwinds propping up the margin line.

## Risks

- **Tariff-refund distortion in the headline beat**: management flagged ~2 percentage points of the 50.1% gross margin and $0.11 of the $2.02 EPS as tariff-refund benefit — normalize for that and gross margin was closer to 48.1% and EPS closer to $1.91, both softer than the reported growth rates suggest.
- **R&D growing faster than revenue**: R&D expense was $11,729M in the quarter versus $8,866M a year earlier, a 32% jump against 16% revenue growth; if AI-related spend (Siri overhaul) doesn't convert to monetizable products, operating leverage erodes.
- **Services/App Store regulatory risk**: one headline explicitly cites App Store revenue falling and guidance disappointing even as the 8-K reports record Services revenue ($30.7B) — the EU's forced App Store fee/rules revamp under the DMA is a live threat to take-rate economics in Apple's highest-margin segment, and the dossier doesn't reconcile these two conflicting readings.
- **Valuation dependent on an unproven 2027 product cycle**: P/E of 35.7x sits well above the 14.2% TTM revenue growth rate, meaning the multiple is effectively pricing in the foldable iPhone and camera AirPods launches that are not slated until 2027 — any slip in that timeline removes the catalyst the current multiple assumes.
- **Thin equity base relative to scale**: shareholders' equity of $107.52B supports $383.27B in total assets and a $4.54T market cap; net income of $128.93B against that equity base implies returns well in excess of 100%, meaning shareholder returns (dividend, buybacks) are funded almost entirely by ongoing cash generation rather than a balance-sheet cushion.
