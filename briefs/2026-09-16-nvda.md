## Thesis

The market is pricing this not as a cyclical windfall but as a durable infrastructure build-out, awarding a $5.11T market cap on the premise that hyperscaler and sovereign AI capex remains at elevated levels long enough to compound the current earnings base meaningfully higher. At P/E 26.8x on already-extraordinary margins, the stock is priced for continued dominance rather than mean reversion.

For the bull case to hold, two things must simultaneously be true: data center capex from the major cloud providers must sustain or grow at a rate that keeps NVIDIA's revenue trajectory from decelerating sharply, and no credible alternative compute architecture (AMD, custom silicon, or otherwise) can erode pricing power enough to compress those 63.7% net margins toward historical semiconductor industry norms. The dossier does not contain forward guidance from the most recent earnings release, so the precise revenue setup the company itself is expecting is absent here — that gap is material.
