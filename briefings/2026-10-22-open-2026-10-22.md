## Talking point of the day

> The most interesting thing on the tape today is that crude is nearing $100 while the front end of the Fed funds curve is pricing 26bp of hikes by January — the market is effectively saying the Fed may have to tighten into a slowing economy, because gold is flat and equities are selling off rather than embracing the oil move as a growth signal. That repricing of the Fed path from cuts to hikes in under three months is the real story; if the 10-year holds above 5.15%, the equity risk premium compression that drove the S&P to 7,700 goes into reverse fast.
